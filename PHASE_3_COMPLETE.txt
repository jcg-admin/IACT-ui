   Purpose: Modal with scale and fade entrance/exit animations
   
   Animation Details:
     • Backdrop: opacity 0→1 (0.2s)
     • Modal: scale 0.9→1, opacity 0→1 (0.3s ease-out)
     • Exit: scale 1→0.9, opacity 1→0 (0.2s ease-in)

       <p>Modal content here</p>
     </ModalAnimation>
   
   Features:
     • Smooth scale + fade animation
     • Click outside to close
     • Stop propagation on modal click
     • Three sizes: sm, md, lg

     • Smooth backdrop fade
